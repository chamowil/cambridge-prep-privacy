Privacy Policy

Cambridge Prep (iOS and Android) · Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Cambridge Prep has no accounts and no server of ours. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. We do not run advertising and we do not use third-party analytics.

What the app stores on your device

Your exam and level choices, practice answers, mistakes, mock-exam results, streaks and XP, writing drafts and speaking recordings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. None of it is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app removes it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Data.

Microphone

Speaking practice records your voice with your device's microphone so you can listen back. Recordings are saved only on your device, are never uploaded or transcribed, and can be deleted from the task or by resetting your progress. The app asks for microphone permission only when you start a speaking task.

Subscriptions

Membership is sold and billed by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only learns from the store whether a subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Access codes

If you were given an access code, it is checked on your device against a list built into the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is intended for learners aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes
